INTERIOR PAINTING.
NOT A DROP SPILLED.

Walls, ceilings, trim and staircases across the Greater Houlton area. We cover what needs covering, we clean up after ourselves, and we are gone when we said we would be.

Interior work is different from exterior work in one obvious way: you are living in it. Somebody has to move your furniture, tape your floors, cut a clean line against your ceiling, and then put the room back the way they found it. Most complaints about interior painters are not about the paint. They are about the mess, the drips, and the crew that vanished for a week halfway through.

That is the part we take seriously. Our customers write about the cleanup as often as they write about the finish.

"They were professional, thoughtful, and knew what they were doing. Not a drop spilled. Perfectionists, all of them, yet quick." Verified customer, Facebook, 2025

WHAT WE PAINT INSIDE

  • Whole-home repaints: upstairs and down, room by room.
  • Walls and ceilings: including the cut-in work around fixtures and corners.
  • Trim, doors and baseboard: the fine lines that make a room look finished.
  • Staircases and hallways: the awkward heights most people would rather not deal with.
  • Wall patching and repair: holes and damage filled and blended before a drop of color goes on.

Patching comes first

Fresh paint over a bad wall is still a bad wall, just a different color. Where a surface needs patching, sanding, or blending, that happens before the color goes on. It is the difference between a room that looks painted and a room that looks new.
