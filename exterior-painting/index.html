EXTERIOR PAINTING
BUILT FOR MAINE WINTERS.

Full exterior repaints, log home restaining, barns, garages, trim and shutters across the Greater Houlton area. Veteran owned. Licensed in the State of Maine.

An exterior paint job in Aroostook County has to survive things a paint job in most of the country never sees. Freeze and thaw, ice, deep snow against the siding for months, then a summer that fades everything it touches. Paint that was applied over a bad surface does not last up here. It lifts, it peels, and you are looking at the same job again in three years.

That is why the prep is the part that matters. Scraping, sanding, and priming bare wood is most of the work on an older Maine home, and it is the part that is easy to skip when someone is quoting you a low number. We do not skip it.

WHAT WE PAINT OUTSIDE

  • Full house repaints: siding, trim, soffits and fascia, start to finish.
  • Log homes: restaining faded and weathered log siding back to a rich, even finish.
  • Barns and outbuildings: large exterior surfaces, painting and staining.
  • Garages and sheds: including bare cedar and previously unfinished siding.
  • Trim, shutters and doors: the detail work that changes how the whole house reads.
  • Fences and lattice: painting and staining.
