HOUSE PAINTERS IN
HOULTON, MAINE.

Veteran owned and operated. Licensed in the State of Maine. We live and work here, and most of our jobs are within a few miles of town.

Houlton is an old town, and it has the housing stock to prove it. Farmhouses, Victorians, Capes, log homes, and plenty of places that have been standing through Aroostook County winters for a century or more. Those houses are beautiful, and they are hard on paint.

We are Freedom Painting. Rodney McEwen runs the crew, we are veteran owned, and we are licensed in the State of Maine. Most of the work we do is right here in and around Houlton, which means when you call us we are not driving up from Bangor and charging you for the trip.

WHAT WE DO AROUND TOWN

  • Exterior repaints: siding, trim, soffits and fascia. See exterior painting.
  • Interior painting: walls, ceilings, trim, staircases, patching. See interior painting.
  • Log home restaining: bringing faded log siding back to an even finish.
  • Barns, garages and outbuildings: the big exterior surfaces nobody else wants.
  • Historic and older homes: scraping, priming, and doing the prep properly on wood that has earned some respect.

OLDER HOMES TAKE MORE PREP

On a hundred year old house, the painting is the easy part. The work is in the scraping, the sanding, the priming, and the patient handling of wood that has been through a lot. Skip that and the job fails in a couple of winters. It is also exactly the corner a cheap quote is cutting.

We have taken on restoration work in town, including the Edward L. Cleveland House here in Houlton. Old buildings are worth doing right.
